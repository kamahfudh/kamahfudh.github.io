Work / Hospitality booking platform

NapzBoxNapzBox

self-check-in for a smart pod hotel.

A booking website for Nginapz, a smart pod and cabin hotel brand under NAPZ Holding, covering search, booking, account, and self-check-in, designed end to end from a client requirement brief.

Role
UI/UX Designer, Freelance
Timeline
Aug 2024 - Oct 2024
Platform
Web
Client
NAPZ Holding Berhad

The context

A new kind of hotel needs a new kind of booking flow.A new kind of hotel needs a new kind of booking flow.

Nginapz positions itself as Malaysia's first smart hotel, built around compact pod and cabin rooms booked and managed entirely through self-service. NAPZ Holding needed a site that could sell that pitch, handle real search-and-book by location and duration, and carry guests through to checking in without a front desk.

What I designed

From homepage to account, one connected flow.From homepage to account, one connected flow.

  • A homepage built to sell the pitch first: hero, a live-style booking widget, and a hotel grid with pricing and availability.
  • Dedicated product pages for the two room types, Nginapz Pod and Nginapz Cabin, each with its own detail and booking path.
  • A four-step "Easy booking" explainer and simplicity, affordability, and technology value cards on the About page.
  • Full account flow: sign-up, email verification, and login with either Google or email and password.

More of the site

Promotions and account, built the same way.Promotions and account, built the same way.

Promo pages reused the same booking and card patterns as the homepage, so vouchers and referrals never felt like a separate part of the product, and the login flow carried the same visual language through to account access.

Outcome

A complete site, handed off screen by screen.A complete site, handed off screen by screen.

Every page, from the homepage down to account verification, was designed and delivered directly from the client's requirements, ready for the development team to build against.
