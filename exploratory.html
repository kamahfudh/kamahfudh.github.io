Exploratory design

Design practice, outside of client work.

A handful of self-directed explorations across e-commerce, streaming, travel, and ticketing, used to try out product spaces and interaction patterns that don't come up in client briefs.

01 - Website

SneakersHub

A sneaker e-commerce storefront concept, exploring a bold product-hero layout and a dark-to-colour gradient theme per product page.

02 - Mobile app

SneakersHub, mobile

The same sneaker storefront as a mobile app, carrying the browse-to-checkout flow from home feed through to product detail.

03 - Mobile app

On The Go

A travel and hotel-booking app concept, from destination search through to room detail and booking.

04 - Mobile app

Toy store

A dark-mode collectibles storefront for Funko Pop figures, from browse through to product detail and cart.

05 - Mobile app

ReadMe

An e-book reader and store concept, balancing an on-going reading list against on-sale and recommended titles.

Weebo

07 - Mobile app

Event For You

An event-discovery and ticketing app, from a categorised event feed through to a scannable digital ticket.
